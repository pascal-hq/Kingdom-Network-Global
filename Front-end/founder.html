Pst. Emmanuel Favour

Transformative Speaker | Author | Leader

Emmanuel Favour is a Kingdom-minded leader and transformative speaker. He is an author, mentor and opinion leader across various fronts; in institutions of higher learning, schools, corporates, ministries and global platforms. Professionally, Emmanuel is an accountant and a strategist by mastery. He is also a minister of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

He is the founder of Kingdom Network Global, a Christian community based organization, focused on transforming lives and impacting generations through evangelism, mentorship, leadership, prayer and the Word of God.

Building Capacity - The Book

In a season where God is raising disruptors and kingdom leaders in the marketplace, this book is a tool that shall shift the language from a place of victim mentality to a place of capacity building. I love the fact that it has touched on the aspect of being young as an advantage.

"The book 'Building Capacity' is an essential rallying call for our generation to plug into the practical requirements and standards necessary to realize and sustain God's purposes in our time."

Emmanuel Favour is an embodiment of what he has written. When you sit down with him all you can see is a man with a burden of a generation who is ready to build capacity at whatever cost for the sake of a generation.

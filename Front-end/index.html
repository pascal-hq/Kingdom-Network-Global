About Kingdom Network Global

Kingdom Network Global is a Christian community based organization that mainly targets the young generation and aims to lead them to the kingdom of God through the Gospel, Leadership, Mentorship, Guidance and Counseling and any other means possible that leads to the Desired end.

Vision

To be the hub for all kingdom possibilities.

Mission

To raise, equip and empower a generation of kings and priests who reign on earth & enhance kingdom advancement agenda.

The Mandate of Kingdom Network

Salvation

The salvation of souls to take part in the end time massive salvation of souls from the kingdom of darkness through evangelism and discipleship.

Equip Believers

To equip and edify believers for the work of the ministry.

Mentorship

Mentor a generation to discover and rediscover their purpose and bring up a Godly generation through mentorship programs, workshops, seminars, trainings and leadership forums.

7 Mountains of Influence

To affect the 7 mountains of influence with the aim of sowing seeds of revival.

Global Impact

To cause global impact to the nations and the globe for the glory of our God through raising change agents.

Community Impact

To impact the community through local school missions, visiting children's homes and local churches.

Our Anchor Pillars

Revelation

Guiding believers into a deeper understanding of God's Word and Kingdom truths.

Manifestation

Demonstrating the power and life of Christ through worship, service, and practical faith.

Experience

Encouraging personal and corporate encounters with God that transform hearts and sustain spiritual growth.

Mentorship School

The Mentorship School is one of the key arms of Kingdom Network Global. It is designed to raise, equip, and empower a generation of young people through intentional mentorship, discipleship, and leadership development.

What We Do

We provide structured mentorship programs that help young people discover their purpose, develop their potential, and walk in their God-given calling. Our approach combines biblical teaching, practical life skills, and leadership training.

How It's Structured

  • High School Mentorship
  • Campus Mentorship
  • Post-Campus & Young Professionals
  • Leadership Forums & Workshops
  • One-on-One Mentorship Pairings

Impact Sessions

Our main programs designed to transform lives and impact generations through the power of the Gospel, worship, and intentional discipleship.

Worship Experiences

Immersive worship gatherings where young people encounter God's presence through praise, worship, prayer, and the Word. These sessions create space for personal transformation and revival.

High School & Campus Missions

Strategic outreach programs in high schools and campuses across Kenya. We engage students through evangelism, mentorship sessions, and leadership training, with impactful sessions like the "Nyambego" series.

Mentorship Masterclasses

In-depth training sessions covering topics like purpose discovery, leadership development, financial literacy, career guidance, and personal growth. These masterclasses are designed to equip young people for life.

Founder & Vision Bearer

Pst. Emmanuel Favour

Emmanuel Favour is a young Kingdom-minded leader and a transformative speaker. He is an Author of the Book Building Capacity, and currently authoring and publishing other books. He is a Mentor of young people especially in highschools, campuses and post campus students across Kenya, with the Kingdom Network School of Mentorship.

He is a University Lecturer, a Financial Consultant and a Revivalist. As a young person, He's also a sought-after youth opinion leader in various circles in faith, corporate, strategy, business, entrepreneurship and related fields.

Emmanuel Favour has a Bachelor's degree in Accounting and doubles up as a Certified Public Accountant (CPA-K). He's also an MBA Holder, specializing in Strategic Management.

He adds up as a Minister of the Gospel of Jesus Christ as an ordained Pastor, since his university days, in the Multimedia University of Kenya.

He is the Founder and Leader of Kingdom Network Global, which is a Christian Community Based Organization, focused on transforming lives and impacting generations through evangelism, mentorship, leadership, prayer and the Word of God.

His Mantra has been, "For God and my generation" because of the burden he has to see young people transformed, and people serving their God given purposes in full potentials. He believes in doing the work of God now, because tomorrow is too far.
